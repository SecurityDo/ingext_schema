Top 10 source IPs by total bandwidth

List the top 10 source IPs by total bandwidth (sent + received) over the last day, excluding log ID 0000000020. Uses _fields.sentdelta/_fields.rcvddelta when present, falling back to sentbyte/rcvdbyte.

NetworkFortigateTraffic
| where TimeGenerated > ago(1d)
| where logid != "0000000020"
| extend sent = coalesce(tolong(_fields.sentdelta), sentbyte),
         rcvd = coalesce(tolong(_fields.rcvddelta), rcvdbyte)
| summarize
    TotalSentBytes = sum(sent),
    TotalRcvdBytes = sum(rcvd),
    EventCount = count(),
    DistinctDsts = dcount(dstip) by srcip
| extend TotalBytes = TotalSentBytes + TotalRcvdBytes
| top 10 by TotalBytes desc
